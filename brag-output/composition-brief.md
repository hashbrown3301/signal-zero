# Hyperframes Composition Brief: iTantra

## Objective
60-second flagship launch film for iTantra (user-requested length, overriding /brag's 15–25 s default).

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output/composition/` (single `index.html`, root id `main`)
- Render: `brag-output/brag.mp4`, landscape 1920x1080, 60 s

## Source material (all real)
- Screen recordings from the 2026-09-29 two-emulator end-to-end run: `talk.mp4` (Hindi phone: Listening → Sending → "66 B sent"), `home.mp4` (Home: "Offline voice link · Models ready · offline"), `packs.mp4` (Language packs, English downloading), `speaking.mp4` (receiving phone, "Speaking…").
- `hi_hook.wav`: the real mic input for the 55 B opening message (3.76 s, 120,336 bytes of PCM). `hi_voice.wav`: the real input for the 66 B message in scene 5.
- `te1/te2/gu2.wav`: the translated lines spoken on the PC by the app's own MMS voice packs (te-listen, gu-listen).
- Fonts bundled in the app: Manrope, Noto Sans per script, JetBrains Mono. Logo geometry from `ui/components/Brand.kt` and the launcher icon gradient.

## Creative direction
Polished, bright studio white, flagship-launch restraint. Idea: voice is heavy, meaning isn't; only meaning travels, so any language can reach any other.

## Truth rules
- App screens are only ever real recordings (the receiving phone is blurred as background, so its untranslated Hindi transcript is illegible).
- Translation is shown only as typography and voices, because it isn't built in the app yet.
- Numbers used: opening 120,336 B (computed from `hi_hook.wav`) → 55 B (17 + 38; seen in the Hindi phone's footage); flagship 66 B (on screen in `talk.mp4`); 10 languages.

## Audio
- Lead: the real voices (Hindi input, then Telugu and Gujarati app voices).
- Bed: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10` from 5.8 s at 0.3, ducked to 0.09 under every voice, faded out at 60 s. Beat lock: the S5 transition at 21.6 s sits on the bed's strong cue (15.82 s of track time).
- SFX: 6 soft ticks as the tiles arrive, a soft impact on "Not bandwidth.", a press click, a packet swish, a soft impact on the logo.
- Audio-reactive visuals: skipped on purpose; the mix is voice-led and the visuals are already busy with real footage.

## Audio v2 (original score)
- `assets/music/score.wav` replaces the stock bed and all separate SFX. It is generated by `composition/score.py` (numpy only; run with the project `.venv`: `python score.py assets/music/score.wav`), scored to this timeline:
  - D-major palette (Dmaj9 · Bm9 · Gmaj9 · Asus). Drone under the hook; pads for the meaning scenes; a 96 BPM groove (kick, offbeat hats, snaps, shaker, sub, ping-pong pluck arp, sidechain pump) for the USP section and the ten-language/on-device run; a breakdown under the conversation; a finale on the logo.
  - Cues on every reveal: glass pops (pitched to the D pentatonic) for text, ticks for bytes and labels, a data-stream of 188 micro-bursts for the codec frames, then a clean bell for iTantra's single packet, shimmers on each translation, whooshes on camera moves, risers into "Transmit meaning", the product reveal and the finale, impacts with chord stabs on section changes.
  - Music ducks ~11 dB inside the stem under every voice.
- Final mix: −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP (ffmpeg volume + alimiter at the poster-bake step).
